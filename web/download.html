How to install on Android

  1. 1
    Download the APK Tap the Download APK button above. If Chrome shows "File might be harmful", tap Download anyway (this is Android's standard prompt when installing directly outside Google Play).
  2. 2
    Allow Unknown Apps Open the downloaded app-debug.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder. If prompted, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".
  3. 3
    Install and Read Tap Install. Ribbon installs cleanly as app.readribbon.debug alongside your regular apps.

What's in this build

✦
Shared Campfire: Organic value-noise fire engine with live embers, hot air wisps, and multiple heat bodies, aligned with the iOS Swift painter.
✦
Persistent Signing Key: Unified debug keystore ensuring seamless updates over subsequent releases without signature mismatch errors.
✦
Bundled Scripture: Full offline text for both the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and World English Bible (WEB).
✦
Quiet Margins & Presence: Shared presence indicators and verse-pinned written or audio note markers.
✦
Targeting Android 16 (API 36): Built natively with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Kotlin Coroutines (minimum Android 13).
